Darkstore Planogram

Lay out your floor on a grid. Each cell is one rack footprint.

Tip: a typical darkstore rack is ~4 ft × 2 ft. A 30 × 20 grid ≈ 120 ft × 40 ft of floor if one cell = one rack.

How to use

Click & drag to paint cells.
Hold Shift to draw a straight row.
Click a placed object to delete it (🗑) —
conveyor/entry/exit also get a rotate (↻).
Right-click drag (or E) to erase.
1–9 switch elements. Cmd/Ctrl+Z undo.
